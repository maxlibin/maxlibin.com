## What is [AI Research Canvas](https://github.com/maxlibin/workspace "AI Research Canvas")?

AI Research Canvas is an innovative platform that combines a dynamic visual workspace with AI-driven capabilities. It enables users to organize data intuitively, collaborate in real-time, and leverage artificial intelligence to gain deeper insights. By integrating these features, the platform aims to make knowledge work smarter, more intuitive, and highly collaborative.

### Key Features:

**Visual Data Organization**: Arrange and manage your research materials on an interactive canvas, allowing for a more intuitive understanding of complex information.

**AI-Powered Editor**: Utilize advanced AI tools to assist in data analysis, summarization, and content generation, streamlining the research process.

**Real-Time Collaboration**: Work simultaneously with team members, sharing insights and making collective progress without the constraints of traditional asynchronous methods.
